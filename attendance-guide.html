Helping Your Child Get to School

Getting to school regularly matters, but every family faces different challenges. You don't need to complete a worksheet to get started. Explore ideas, try what fits your family, and reach out to your child's school when you need support.

What would be helpful right now?

One idea is enough to start

Choose something realistic for your family. Talk with your child about what worked and what needs to change. If the concern continues, contact your child's school so you can problem-solve together.

These are general family suggestions. Your child's school can help with specific attendance concerns, transportation questions, and available supports.
