    How to know whether it's helping

    These guides offer general parenting ideas, not individualized clinical advice. If someone is in immediate danger, seek urgent help. If your child is experiencing ongoing distress or safety concerns, contact an appropriate professional or school support team.

    Start with a conversation

    You do not have to solve every challenge alone. Your child's teacher, school counselor, or school team can help you understand what's happening and identify support. Share what you are seeing at home and ask what the school is noticing.
