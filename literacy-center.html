When reading or writing is hard

Children can struggle for different reasons. Ask the teacher what specific skill is difficult, what the school has assessed, what instruction or intervention is being provided, and how progress will be shared. Difficulty with word reading is different from difficulty understanding a text; each calls for appropriate support.

For families of older children, reading difficulties still deserve direct attention. Do not assume a middle or high school student will simply grow out of them.
