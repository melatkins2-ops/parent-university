When your child is having trouble reading or writing

Children can struggle for different reasons. Ask your child's teacher which parts of reading are going well, where your child needs help, what support the school is giving, and how you'll know whether it's working. Difficulty with word reading is different from difficulty understanding a text; each calls for appropriate support.

For families of older children, reading difficulties still deserve direct attention. Do not assume a middle or high school student will simply grow out of them.
